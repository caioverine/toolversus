Frequently Asked Questions

How often do you update tool information?

We update pricing and features regularly, usually monthly or when we become aware of significant changes.

Can I suggest a new SaaS tool?

Absolutely! Send us an email at contact@toolversus.co with the tool name, website, and brief description. We'd love to add it.

Do you offer affiliate links?

We do work with some SaaS tools through affiliate partnerships. These help us keep the service free. We always disclose when links are affiliate links.

Is ToolVersus completely free?

Yes! ToolVersus is 100% free to use. We're supported by ads and affiliate links, but the comparison tool itself will always be free.

Can I use ToolVersus data on my website?

Please contact us at contact@toolversus.co to discuss licensing or data usage options.
